Overview

  1. A prediction market platform (inspired by Polymarket) where users buy and sell Yes / No outcome shares on future events.
  2. Markets span many categories — Politics, Finance, Sports, Crypto, Tech, Geopolitics, Culture, World.
  3. Share prices reflect the crowd's estimated probability: a "Yes 51¢" share pays $1 if the event resolves Yes, so the price ≈ implied probability.
  4. Wallet-based trading — connect a wallet, take a position, and each market resolves to Yes / No and settles.
  5. Sole developer — designed, built, and operated the product end-to-end: market design, smart contracts, web app, and deployment.

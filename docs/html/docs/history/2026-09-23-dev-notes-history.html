Tech #72: tokenized compute is a forward contract on a perishable good

  • Cause: jay pasted one line — "JUST IN: BlackRock says AI compute could be tokenized in the future" — and asked for it as a Tech item. No URL came with it.
  • Reasoning: restating the headline would have added nothing, so the card is built on what the headline hides. Compute is not becoming a new asset class; it is being reclassified from a service into a financial claim, and a GPU-hour is perishable, non-fungible and location-bound. Electricity and airline seats are the precedents, and both needed decades of contract engineering — because forward markets in perishables have never failed at issuance, they fail at deliverable grade, delivery point, preemption, and settlement on failure. The load-bearing word in BlackRock's framing is collateral: an asset manager cares that compute becomes financeable, but collateral implies liquidation, which here means finding someone to take physical delivery of GPU-hours they do not want at the worst possible moment.

Apply penalty logic

One of our biggest advantages over other prediction markets is that anyone can create a market. However, to prevent abuse, we require creators to deposit tokens. If an admin determines that a market violates our policy, this deposit will be forfeited. Our strictest rules prohibit content involving violence, sexual abuse, drugs, or child exploitation. If a creator doesn't follow the rules, their funds will be forfeited. However, if they voluntarily close the market within a specified time after receiving an admin warning, there is no penalty.

Some markets may be controversial or extreme without strictly violating our policies. Rather than judging these contrally, we rely on social consensus. If users find value in a market, they will trade in it. If not, the market will naturally fade into obscurity and will not appear on the landing page or the related categories. We currently use trading volume as the primary filter for visibility and pland to adopt more sophisticated algorithms in the future.

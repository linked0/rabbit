Created a new study folder `ai/litellm-routing/` (matching the `mlx-study/` convention:
numbered scripts + Korean README). `01_unified_completion.py` calls one `ask()` against

### math Day 3/50: limits & continuity
Added `math/day03_limits_continuity.py` + README section, matching the Day 2 pattern.
Covers three threads: continuous compounding `(1+r/n)^n → e^r`, continuity
`lim_{x→a} f(x) = f(a)`, and the probabilistic limit `f^k → 0` (DAS sampling soundness).
Exercise confirmed by run: `e^0.4 ≈ 1.4918`, and `f^k` (f=0.5) drops below 1e-6 at k=20.

How It Works

My process.

A focused, collaborative process that moves fast without cutting corners. Most engagements go from kickoff to handoff in 6–8 weeks.

01
Discover

We dig into your users, your market, and your business goals. I ask the questions your team is too close to ask.

02
Define

Together we map out user flows, information architecture, and the core jobs-to-be-done that will drive every design decision.

03
Design

From low-fi wireframes to high-fidelity screens. Iterative, feedback-driven, and always with your engineers in mind.

04
Deliver

Fully annotated Figma files, a design system, and an optional dev-handoff session so nothing gets lost in translation.

Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Flowdesk strips out the complexity of enterprise helpdesks and gives small teams exactly what they need to deliver great support, fast.

📬

Shared team inbox

One unified inbox for all support emails. Every teammate sees every conversation — no more lost threads or silent handoffs.

🚦

Collision detection

See when a teammate is already typing a reply. Never send a double-response or step on each other's toes again.

⚡

Canned reply library

Build a library of your best answers. Ship replies in seconds — and edit before sending so they always feel human.

🎯

Smart routing rules

Route conversations by keyword, sender domain, or round-robin. Every ticket lands with exactly the right person.

👀

Real-time presence

See who's active in the queue right now. Coordinate your support rotation without a single Slack ping.

🔗

Slack & Gmail integrations

Connect your existing email in minutes. Get notified in Slack. Your team meets Flowdesk where they already work.

How it works

Up and running in 5 minutes flat

No training sessions. No IT tickets. Connect your inbox and you're live.

1

Connect your inbox

Forward your support address to Flowdesk or connect Gmail directly. New emails appear in your shared queue instantly.

2

Assign & collaborate

Claim conversations, drop internal notes for teammates, and set routing rules — zero configuration required to get started.

3

Resolve faster

Reply with canned answers, close tickets, and watch your queue shrink. Your customers get answers. You get your sanity back.

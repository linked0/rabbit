# Clarifying Questions for Sarah Chen's Landing Page

**Before I start building, I have a few quick questions:**

**About your work and process:**
1. What does your design process look like — do you have a specific number of phases (e.g., discovery → wireframes → prototyping → handoff)? Having named stages makes the "Process" section much more compelling.
2. Do you have 2–4 case studies or portfolio pieces you'd like to highlight? If so, what startups did you work with, and what was the outcome (e.g., "helped X launch in 8 weeks," "increased conversion by 40%")?

**About your audience and goals:**
3. When you say "book a call," do you use a specific scheduling tool like Calendly, cal.com, or something else? This determines how the CTA button should link.
4. Do you have a preferred contact method or intake form tool (Typeform, Google Form, native HTML form), or should I build a static HTML form with mailto?

**About social proof:**
5. Do you have any testimonials from past clients, or notable startup names / logos you've worked with that you're allowed to feature?
6. Do you have any standout numbers to show (e.g., "10+ MVPs launched," "5 years experience," "startups raised $X after launch")?

**About visual identity:**
7. Do you have a logo or wordmark, or should I use a typographic logo ("Sarah Chen" styled in the header)?
8. Any typeface preferences, or are you happy with a clean system/sans-serif stack?
